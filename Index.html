Fixed campaign assumptions and calculation rules
Read-only assumptions. Gross earnings use 12 earning days.
Level Campaign Cost One-Time Activation Fee Ads/Day Earnings/Click Gross Campaign Earnings
  • Required initial funding = first-cycle campaign purchases + first-cycle activation fees.
  • Multiple campaigns at the same newly activated level incur only one activation fee.
  • Gross campaign earnings = ads/day × earnings/click × 12 days.
  • Cycle profit = gross earnings − campaign purchases − activation fees. Withdrawal fees are shown separately.
  • Later cycles select the affordable combination of up to three campaigns with the highest gross earnings minus purchase costs, matching the original planner's selection rule. Activation fees are included when checking affordability.
  • Cash before withdrawal = opening cash − campaign outlay + gross earnings.
  • Actual gross withdrawal = the smaller of the withdrawal request and available cash. Net received = actual gross withdrawal − the 10% fee.
  • Cash after withdrawal funds the next cycle. Unspent cash carries forward; no borrowing or additional deposits occur.
  • Cash reserve = cash after withdrawal − next cycle outlay. The final row has no next-cycle funding commitment.
  • Funding surplus/(shortfall) = current gross earnings − actual gross withdrawal − next cycle outlay. A negative amount means carried cash helps fund the next cycle.
  • Months use a 365/12-day average month. Money is calculated in integer cents, with withdrawal fees rounded to the nearest cent.
  • If all first-cycle campaigns are set to None, initial funding is zero and no campaigns run.

Illustrative projection using fixed assumptions, not a guarantee of earnings. Inputs reset when the page reloads.
